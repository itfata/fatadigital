05 / Questions & answers

Before we begin.

The essentials about development and collaboration. We agree the details of your project directly.

How much does a website cost?

Landing with 1 page starts at $150, Business website with up to 5 pages at $500, and Pro with up to 8 pages at $800. Each package includes 2 rounds of revisions. Pro also includes two language versions (UA/EN) and enquiries sent to a CRM or Google Sheets. Additional pages beyond the package cost $100 each. Online store pricing is discussed individually. The final scope and price are agreed before work begins.

How long does development take?

Depending on scope, a small project may take 3–5 days or around a week, while a platform may take approximately 2–3 weeks. We agree the final timeline after discussing functionality and the availability of content.

What do you need to get started?

Tell us about your business, audience, website goals and required features. Share any existing logo, copy, photos or reference websites. We will clarify the materials and next steps together.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. Mobile and tablet layouts are included. We check readability, navigation and key actions at different screen sizes.

Do you help with domains and hosting?

Yes, we help connect your domain and hosting. The provider, service fees and account access are discussed before launch.

Can the website be updated after launch?

Yes. Pages, content and features can be added or updated. Post-launch support costs $30–50 per month, with the scope agreed separately. Each additional page beyond the package costs $100.

Can you connect a CRM or Telegram?

Yes, we work with enquiries sent to Telegram, Google Sheets, email and CRM systems. We clarify the service, access requirements and integration options before estimating the work.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes, we work with clients in other countries. Fata Digital operates in Ukraine. We agree the communication language, working arrangements and available payment method directly before work begins.
